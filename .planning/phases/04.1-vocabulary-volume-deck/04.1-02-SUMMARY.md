M23 and M24 are the discriminating pair: each fires its own ceiling while the
other survives, so neither is a restatement of the other. M24 is the concrete
case the second ceiling exists for — a bigram ceiling alone would have passed it.

**M25 is the one the phase turns on.** Twenty cards whose examples are natural
English, contain their headwords, run seven-plus words, are all on the NGSL, in
rank order, with no duplicate anywhere — and it produces **exactly three
failures, all of them frame-diversity**. Flat prose that passes every other check
is caught by these three assertions and by nothing else in the harness.

**One honest limitation, for plan 07.** The distinct-shape floor
(`≥ ceil(n/5)`) cannot fire *alone* at n = 20: fewer than 4 distinct shapes over
20 cards forces some shape to hold ≥ 7, which trips the bigram ceiling of 2
first. It becomes independently falsifiable around n ≥ 40 and is genuinely
independent at n = 500, where 20 shapes of 25 would satisfy the ceiling and fail
the floor. At twenty cards it is dominated; it is not decoration, it is early.

## Things the plan or the locked decisions got wrong

1. **`verify-scenario-content.mts:389-396` is now `401-416`.** The twelve import
   lines this plan appended shifted the four parse tripwires down. The
   assertions are **byte-identical** — proved by diffing the block against
   `HEAD` and by the plan's own removed-line check, which found **zero**
   non-comment lines removed across 521 additions — but every future document
   citing those line numbers is now citing the wrong ones. Cite them by their
   text (`a scenario key the curriculum does not have parses to nothing`), not
   by line.
2. **272.8 B/id is an average, not a marginal cost.** A volume id costs
   **220.6 B** measured. See the payload section above. CONTEXT's table is
   conservative in the safe direction, so nothing was decided wrongly on it, but
   the figure should not be re-extrapolated a third time — the harness prints it.
3. **The plan's Task 1 second `<automated>` check only makes sense after the
   fixture is regenerated.** Run in the order the plan lists it, it fails by
   design: `--update` legitimately rewrites the fixture the first time. Run it
   as an *idempotence* check, after the regeneration, which is what it is.
4. **§D's `calls coreVocabItemId(` clause was already corrected in `1085d83`
   and the correction is right.** Confirmed against the shipped bank: it has no
   such call, stores the bare slug and does not import the key space. The
   original wording would have failed a correct bank.
5. **The scope loop's "two positive branches" resolve to a prefix-routed
   dispatch, and one consequence is worth stating.** An id that is neither a
   well-formed volume id nor a scheduled scenario kind fails on the **scenario**
   label, because anything not starting with `vocab:` routes there. That is the
   honest behaviour of a router and it still fails — but a reader expecting
   "fails on both labels" will not see that.
6. **The 352 figure is derived, so nothing tripwires a shrinking corpus.** The
   duplicate check reads the banks, and the count appears only inside an
   assertion label. This follows the project's derived-coverage rule, and the
   trade is recorded here rather than discovered later: deleting scenario
   vocabulary would quietly loosen the volume tier's duplicate check.
7. **CONTEXT's `<quality_floor>` item 4 says "none against the 352 scenario
   vocabulary terms" and the corpus is 352 total AND 352 distinct** — measured.
   The two numbers agreeing is luck, not structure; the assertion uses a Set.

## Deviations from plan

**1. [Deliberate, and it exceeds the plan] Mutations were run here, not deferred
to plan 07.** The plan's `<verification>` section says mutations are plan 07's
job and that this plan owes only a written list. The list is written above — and
all 45 were also executed, in a scratch worktree, with the results recorded.
Nothing about plan 07's job changes: it re-runs them against **final** content,
where the frame ceilings will be 25/100/100 rather than 2/4/4 and the distinct
floor becomes independently falsifiable.

**2. [Rule 3 — blocking] The mutation driver had to normalise line endings.**
The worktree checks out CRLF under `core.autocrlf`, so multi-line find strings
matched nothing and four mutations reported `SETUP-ERROR` rather than a result.
The driver now normalises to LF for matching and restores the original bytes
verbatim afterwards; the worktree's `git status` was empty at the end.

**3. [Judgement call] `saturatedIds` was renamed rather than aliased.** The plan
says to "name the variable for what it is". Aliasing `saturatedIds = storageIds`
would have satisfied the letter and left the misleading name in the two places
that build the state.
