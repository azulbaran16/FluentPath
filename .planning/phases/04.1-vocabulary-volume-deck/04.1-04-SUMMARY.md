## Things the plan or the gates got wrong

1. **`color` (378) is glossed `"color / tono"` and that is a fudge, recorded as
   one.** The gate asserts `es !== word`, and Spanish for *color* is *color*. Three
   other cognates in this batch were glossed around honestly — `local` →
   "del barrio / de la zona", `real` → "auténtico / de verdad", `idea` →
   "ocurrencia" — because each has a real Spanish synonym. `color` does not, so
   the card satisfies the assertion by appending a near-synonym rather than by
   having a distinct front. **The gate is doing its job and the content is
   working around it.** The honest options are all worse: skipping needs a fourth
   skip reason (a decision, not a convenience), and a gloss of "tono" alone would
   have a learner answer *tone*. Plans 05 and 06 will hit more of these below
   rank 500 (`total`, `natural`, `general`, `personal`, `similar`), and somebody
   should decide whether a `cognate` skip reason is worth declaring **before** a
   plan needs it, the way `already-taught` was declared at plan 01 and first used
   at plan 03.
2. **The harness's printed "top ten openings" is a real signal at x2 and is
   presented as if it were not.** Plan 03 recorded it as "nearly useless" because
   it was ten ties at 1; this batch's first run put **four bigrams at x2** and all
   four were genuine repeated frames worth rewriting. They cannot fail — the
   ceiling is 13 — so the line is a *warning nobody is told to read*. The fix is
   one line in the harness: print the top ten opening WORDS beside the top ten
   shapes, and mark any shape at x2 or more. Until then, every authoring plan has
   to be told in prose to read a line the harness prints without comment.
3. **The plan's Task 1 idempotence check is still listed before the scan**, the
   same defect plan 02 recorded as its item 3 and plan 03 as its item 2, now
   present in a third plan verbatim — and this time the plan carries an
   `<ordering_correction>` block at the top of `<tasks>` that fixes it in prose
   while leaving the `<verify>` block wrong. **Plans 05 and 06 carry the same
   pair.** A correction that does not edit the thing it corrects will be followed
   by whoever reads the block and missed by whoever reads the checks.
4. **Rank completeness landing on 378 exactly is arithmetic, not luck, and it is
   worth saying so** because a reader may take `260 + 118 = 378` as a coincidence
   worth checking. It is forced: every rank at or below the deepest one is either
   carded or declared, so the two counts must sum to the deepest rank. The number
   to check is not the sum — it is that the *deepest rank* is what the harness
   derived (378) and not what this document claims.
5. **The deck-browser overlap grew 3 → 4 and has now produced its first identical
   front.** Plan 03 predicted the figure would climb and that somebody would read
   a growing unasserted number as a problem. It is still not one — but the *kind*
   of overlap changed here, from "same word, different sense" to "same word, same
   gloss", and that distinction is not visible in the printed count. If a later
   plan wants a tripwire, the thing to count is identical glosses, not identical
   words.
