The phase gate. Nothing new is built here; everything built is proved, and what is not proved is
written down by name.

**This plan is shaped around CONT-04's lesson.** Phase 4 closed with 14,577 assertions, 2,021
id-stability assertions, an independent enumeration and a 19-mutation sweep — and **the browser pass
was never performed**, because the reviewer's context was exhausted before it could be driven. Ten
things nobody has ever seen are open as WINDOWS 62, including the batched recall deck, which is the

automated half always finishes and the human half is always last.

without it, and hands the reviewer an ordered checklist with URLs rather than a request to "check it
works".

she said too little content. She was right by an order of magnitude, and it was the second time she was
right about something no gate caught. Five hundred cards is the answer to what she said. This plan is
where somebody finds out whether the answer is any good.

Purpose: prove the gates, see the thing, and record the debts.
Output: a mutation sweep, a reader pass, a browser verdict, and the project's rules updated.

## Assert that a retired id cannot inflate the volume surface

Plan 05 retired `vocab:color`, so the app can now hold an `srs` entry under a `vocab:` key **no
bank emits** — any learner who rated that card before it was retired has one. `mergeProgress`
unions keys blindly and cannot delete one, so this state is permanent and will recur with every
future retirement.

**Verified by inspection at 04.1-05, and that is exactly why it needs an assertion.** Today it
holds for the right reason: `CoreVocabView`'s `metCount` iterates `ALL_ITEMS` (the bank) and asks
whether each is in `srs`, rather than counting `srs` keys with a `vocab:` prefix; `dueCount`
filters `dueReviewIds()` through `VOLUME_IDS`; and `resolveCoreVocabItem` returns `undefined`.
All three are bank-driven, which is the derived-coverage rule holding.

**None of it is asserted.** A future refactor that counts `srs` keys by prefix — the obvious
"simplification" — would inflate "you've met" above the deck's own size and nobody would notice.

Add to the phase gate:

1. Over a synthetic `ProgressState` whose `srs` holds a retired `vocab:` key alongside live ones,
   the surface's met and due counts equal the counts over the live ids alone.
2. `resolveCoreVocabItem` returns `undefined` for every id in the fixture's `retired` list.
3. Source-level: `CoreVocabView` derives its counts from the bank and **never filters `srs` keys by
   string prefix** — the check that catches the refactor before it ships.

Aim a mutation at each, and require it CAUGHT on its own label.
</retired_orphan_assertion>
