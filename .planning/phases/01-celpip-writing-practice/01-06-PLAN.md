  <name>Task 2: Human verification — full CELPIP Writing flow against the five success criteria</name>
  <what-built>The full CELPIP Writing section: free `/celpip` landing with Task 1 / Task 2 libraries, per-task attempt status, disabled coming-soon tabs, attempt history, and a mobile notice; and the `/celpip/writing/[taskId]` simulator with a real countdown, word counter, draft autosave, timer-expiry lock, side-by-side model-answer results, and a descriptor-based self-evaluation checklist. ~16 original tasks (~8 Task 1 emails + ~8 Task 2 surveys).</what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Run `npm run dev` and open http://localhost:3000/celpip.
    1. Landing: confirm the page loads WITHOUT signing in; Task 1 and Task 2 tabs switch card grids; Speaking/Reading/Listening tabs are visibly disabled with a "Coming soon" badge; on a narrow window the tabs wrap instead of clipping; on a phone-width viewport the desktop-recommendation notice appears and is dismissible.
    2. Timed attempt: open any Task 1 task, click "Start Timed Attempt", type past 150 words and confirm the counter shows "in range" (sky); type past 200 and confirm the over-range warning color (submission still allowed). Refresh the tab mid-writing and confirm your text is restored (autosave).
    3. Timer expiry (fastest check: pick a task and let it run, OR temporarily verify with a short limit if the executor exposes one): confirm that near the end the timer turns red at ≤2 minutes, and at 0 the editor locks, the "Time's up" banner shows, and BOTH "Submit as-is" and "Continue untimed" are offered. Choose "Continue untimed" and confirm your text is intact and editing resumes.
    4. Practice mode: start "Start Practice" and confirm Pause/Resume works.
    5. Results + self-check: submit and confirm your text renders beside the original model answer in scrollable panels, the rubric checklist is grouped by the four dimensions with explanations, and checking a subset (not all) is allowed. Confirm attempt metrics (time used, words) show.
    6. History persistence: go back to /celpip, confirm the attempt appears in history (date, duration, word count) and the task card status updated; reload the browser and confirm the attempt is still there.
    7. Autosave failure (optional, if reproducible): in a private/incognito window with storage blocked, confirm the "couldn't save your draft" warning appears and your typed text is NOT lost.
    8. IP spot-check: skim two model answers and confirm they read as original prose (no copied exam text).
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" if all seven checks pass, or list the specific failures (screen, step, expected vs actual) to feed gap closure.</resume-signal>
