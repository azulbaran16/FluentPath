Complete the Speaking section: eight original prompts covering the exam's eight task shapes,
the full four-dimension self-evaluation rubric, and the executable content harness the rest
of the phase will extend.

prompt is about a tenth of the authoring cost of a Reading part and about a thirtieth of a
Listening set — and it closes a whole requirement. It is sequenced here for exactly that
reason: the calendar buys the most requirement-coverage per hour right now, and the beta
user gets a section she can rehearse repeatedly against a single prompt.

validation gap RESEARCH identified, created here alongside its first real subject.

task shapes, correct timings, recording, playback, self-evaluation, and sync. Reading and
Listening are honestly marked unavailable on the landing.
