<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 2: Human verification of the six phase success criteria</name>
  <what-built>
    Three new CELPIP sections at `/celpip` — Reading, Listening and Speaking — plus a
    skill-aware landing whose coverage claims are derived from the content banks rather than
    written by hand. Attempts in all three sections persist through the same server-side
    contract Writing uses.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Do all of this against a production build (`npm run build && npm start`), not `npm run dev`.
    Two of the six criteria involve behaviour that only a real build exhibits.

    **Criterion 1 — Reading.** Open `/celpip`, pick the Reading set, work through it. Confirm
    the timer is armed **per part** and re-arms as you advance, and that the allowances match
    the exam: 11 minutes for correspondence, 8 for the diagram, 9 for information, 11 for
    viewpoints. Confirm you meet all four part shapes, that three of the four put
    fill-in-the-blank items inside a reply, a message or a reader comment, and that the diagram
    part shows a real point-form stimulus. Submit with one question deliberately left blank —
    it must let you submit. On the results screen confirm every wrong answer shows both the
    correct option and an explanation.

    **Criterion 2 — Listening.** Start the Listening set. Confirm the audio check runs first.
    Confirm you never see the script text before answering, that no question is visible until
    playback has finished, and that questions then arrive **one at a time in audio order** with
    no way to go back and change an earlier answer. Take notes while listening. After
    submitting, confirm you get an explained key and the transcript with speaker labels. In the
    three-speaker discussion part, confirm you can tell the speakers apart.

    **Criterion 3 — Speaking.** Open a Speaking prompt. Confirm the prep window is silent and
    that **recording starts on its own** when prep expires — you should not have to press
    anything — and that the two windows match the task (30 then 90 seconds for tasks 1 and 7,
    60 then 60 for tasks 5 and 6, 30 then 60 for the rest). Then **play the recording back —
    you must hear yourself.** Confirm the operating system microphone indicator goes out when
    recording stops, not when you leave the page. Complete the self-evaluation checklist with
    only some items ticked and finish the attempt.

    **Criterion 4 — persistence.** Signed in, complete one attempt in each of the three
    sections. Open the app in a different browser, sign in as the same account, and confirm all
    three attempts are there. Then confirm your **existing Writing attempt history is still
    complete and unchanged** — that is live data and it is the thing this phase most had to
    avoid breaking.

    **Criterion 5 — honesty.** Read the `/celpip` landing's coverage lines out loud. For each
    one, ask: can she actually do that today? Anything the app cannot do must be stated, not
    omitted. Specifically confirm the landing says that the describe-a-scene Speaking task uses
    a written scene rather than a picture, and that it states the true number of Listening part
    shapes available.

    **Criterion 6 — originality.** Read at least one complete Reading passage, one complete
    Listening script, three Speaking prompts and the whole Speaking self-evaluation rubric in
    full. You are looking for anything that reads as if it were lifted rather than written for
    this app. This phase had access to official format documents and to the official level
    descriptors, which Phase 1's review did not, so the rubric deserves particular attention:
    every descriptor must be our own sentence expressing the same idea, not the source's
    sentence with words swapped. The automated grep in task 1 catches source names; only a
    person catches close paraphrase.

    **Phone pass — do at least one full section on a phone.** That is where the recorder
    container probe, the voice fallback and the silent-switch guidance all matter, and it is
    where the beta user is most likely to practise on the bus.
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" or list, per criterion, exactly what failed and on which device</resume-signal>
