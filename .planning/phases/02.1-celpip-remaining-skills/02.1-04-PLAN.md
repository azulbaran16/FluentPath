<objective>
Build the plumbing the Listening section stands on: the content types, the persistence field,
the single place `speechSynthesis` is driven, and the audio check that is this section's
failure valve.

Purpose: D-04 forbids showing the script and D-05 hides the questions until playback ends,
which means a learner whose audio silently fails has nothing on screen and no way forward.
Every browser landmine in this section — Chrome truncating a long utterance at roughly
fifteen seconds with no error, iOS rejecting a `speak()` that is not part of a user gesture,
`getVoices()` being empty on first call, Safari wedging its synthesizer when backgrounded
mid-utterance — is handled once, here, rather than four times across components.

Output: `src/lib/celpip-speech.ts` as the one speech idiom for this section, an
`AudioCheck` step that anchors the first utterance in a deliberate press, listening content
types shaped so recorded audio can drop in later without a migration (D-03), and
`listeningAttempts` through the persistence contract.

**If execution stops right after this plan:** nothing new is visible to the learner. This
plan is deliberately not shippable on its own — plan 05 is the one that makes Listening
usable, and it is small because this plan exists. If the calendar is tight, plans 04 and 05
should be treated as one unit.
</objective>

    point without a timer hack; it gives multi-speaker parts genuinely distinct voices, which
    is what those parts actually test; it makes the post-answer transcript renderable with
    speaker labels for free; and it leaves an obvious per-segment slot for recorded audio when

  <files>src/lib/celpip-speech.ts, src/components/celpip/AudioCheck.tsx</files>
  <read_first>src/components/practice/ListeningRoom.tsx (the repo's existing speech idiom, including the cancel-before-speak rule and the onend handoff), src/components/practice/PronunciationLab.tsx (feature-detect-and-ref shape, and how it distinguishes a denied permission from a generic failure), .planning/phases/02.1-celpip-remaining-skills/02.1-RESEARCH.md (Pattern 1, Pattern 2, and Pitfalls 2, 3 and 5)</read_first>
  <action>
    Create `src/lib/celpip-speech.ts` as the single place this section drives
    `speechSynthesis`. Everything the four existing call sites in the app do stays true here:
    guard with a combined `typeof window === "undefined" || !window.speechSynthesis` check so
    the module is SSR-safe and feature-detected in one expression; call `cancel()` before
    `speak()`, always; keep `lang` at en-US and `rate` at 0.95, the values used everywhere
    else in the app.

    Export a voice hook that reads `getVoices()` once at mount, filters to English, subscribes
    to `voiceschanged`, and unsubscribes plus `cancel()`s on teardown — a component that does
    not cancel on unmount keeps talking over the next page. `getVoices()` is frequently empty
    on the first call because the list populates asynchronously, so voice selection must never
    be awaited and must never gate playback.

    Export a speaker-to-voice assignment that is a **preference, never a requirement**. Where
    distinct English voices exist, assign one per distinct speaker name deterministically so
    the same script sounds the same on every run. Where they do not — and at least one source
    reports Safari returning nothing usable — fall through to the browser default and
    distinguish speakers by a small per-speaker `pitch` offset plus the on-screen label the
    turn already carries. Never block playback on a missing voice.

    Export the play function. It takes the ordered turns and callbacks for started, completed
    and failed. It calls `cancel()`, then constructs one `SpeechSynthesisUtterance` per turn
    and hands **every one of them to `speak()` synchronously inside the same call**, because
    `speechSynthesis` is a queue and iOS Safari rejects a `speak()` that is not part of a user
    gesture — a call chained from a previous utterance's `onend` is no longer part of one. The
    first utterance's `onstart` reports started, the last utterance's `onend` reports
    completed, and any `onerror` reports failed. That last-`onend` signal is the only thing
    that may reveal the questions (D-05), so it must be attached to the final utterance and
    nowhere else.

    Export an explicit cancel, and a small availability probe the components can render an
    unsupported state from.

    Do **not** implement pause. On Android `pause()` is implemented as cancel and the `paused`
    flag is not reliably updated, so no product requirement may rest on it. Practice mode
    pauses the *timer*, which `Timer` already supports, and the UI must say that audio itself
    cannot be paused rather than letting it surprise her. Do not use `onboundary` for any
    progress indicator — it is not fired on Android and is unsupported for many voices.

    Then create `src/components/celpip/AudioCheck.tsx`, the unscored warm-up step that
    mirrors the exam's own unscored practice task and doubles as this section's failure
    valve. It plays a short original line through the driver from a deliberate button press —
    which is what anchors the first utterance in a user gesture at the exact moment the
    learner is paying attention to whether sound came out — and then offers one explicit
    branch: heard it, or heard nothing. The "heard nothing" branch must lead somewhere real:
    a retry, a plain-language note that on an iPhone the hardware silent switch mutes Safari
    audio while other apps still make sound, and — because this is practice, not an exam — an
    escape that reveals the transcript rather than stranding her. Nothing here is scored and
    the timer is not armed until this step is passed or explicitly skipped.

    Follow the design tokens: no hard-coded hex anywhere, Tailwind utilities for static
    colours and inline `var(--...)` for computed ones, `rounded-[var(--radius)]` and
    `shadow-[var(--shadow-soft)]` on card panels, `--sky` as the CELPIP accent, headings in
    `font-display`. A literal colour breaks dark mode silently.
  </action>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser speech engine -> page | A platform singleton shared with every other page in the tab; leaving it speaking or wedged affects the rest of the app |
| browser -> `/api/celpip-progress` | Listening answers cross into a validated write against live learner data |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02.1-16 | Denial of service | `speechSynthesis` singleton left speaking or wedged | medium | mitigate | `cancel()` before every play and on unmount; the voice hook cancels on teardown; backgrounding is handled by cancelling rather than pretending playback continued |
| T-02.1-17 | Denial of service | Chrome truncating a long utterance with no error | high | mitigate | One utterance per speaker turn, with a per-turn word ceiling enforced by the content harness in plan 05; chunking supersedes the `resume()`-every-14-seconds workaround, which is a timer fighting the engine and misbehaves on Android |
| T-02.1-18 | Denial of service | learner with no working audio, stranded by D-04/D-05 | high | mitigate | `AudioCheck` runs before the timer arms; its "heard nothing" branch offers retry, plain-language guidance, and a transcript escape |
| T-02.1-19 | Tampering | `listeningAttempts` merged by a non-associative rule | critical | mitigate | Reuses the existing `unionRecord` plus canonical-entries rule already proven by the O(n^3) sweep; new fixtures added to `celpipStates` so the proof covers the new field |
| T-02.1-20 | Tampering | prototype pollution via answer-map keys | medium | mitigate | `answers` goes through `sanitizedRecord`, which assembles a fresh object and skips poisoned keys |
| T-02.1-21 | Denial of service | unbounded growth from persisted notes | medium | mitigate | Notes are not persisted at all; the attempt record holds only bounded numbers, booleans and an integer answer map, keeping the 2 MiB body cap headroom proved in plan 01 intact |
| T-02.1-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | Zero packages. A TTS wrapper is explicitly named in RESEARCH as a scope change requiring justification against the empty legitimacy audit |
