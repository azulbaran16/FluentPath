    The part tests attribution. Write the positions so they overlap partially — two people
    agreeing on the problem and disagreeing on the remedy is harder and more realistic than
    two people disagreeing about everything. At least four questions must require the learner
    to say who held a given position rather than merely what the position was.

    The reader-comment blanks are where the register work happens: the correct option agrees
    or disagrees with a specific named position in a way that fits both the facts of the
    article and the commenter's stance, and the wrong options fit the sentence but attribute
    to the wrong person or reverse the stance.

    article that settles it. Ids unique across the set. D-06 originality throughout.
