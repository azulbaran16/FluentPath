<objective>
Make Reading real: the correspondence part and the information part of Reading set 1, plus
the harness invariants and the landing wiring.

Purpose: the correspondence part is the one that must ship first, because it is the only part
that exercises both question types at once — comprehension items on the first email and
drop-down blanks inside the reply, where the right option has to fit both the facts of the
first email and the reply's tone. The information part is the one that carries the
"not stated in the text" style option, which is what makes the real thing hard. Between them
the learner meets both of the section's difficulties.

Output: 20 items across two of the exam's four reading parts, Reading offered as a real
section on the landing, and the reading invariants gated by the content harness.

**If execution stops right after this plan:** the learner has practice in all three new
skills — eight Speaking task shapes, five Listening part shapes, and two Reading parts
including the drop-down-blank type. That is the point in the phase where every requirement
has real content behind it.
</objective>

    `READING_SETS` in the barrel. Structure the module so that adding a part is a pure append

    droppable without touching anything else.

    Author the `correspondence` part: an original email of roughly 200 to 250 words in an
    everyday-Canadian context, plus a reply of similar length. Set `minutes` to 11. **Eleven
    items in a confirmed split: the first six are information multiple-choice questions on the
    email, and the last five are fill-in-the-blank items inside the reply.** That six-then-five
    order is how the real part is laid out, per the beta user's official format material, and
    it is not interchangeable — she should meet the blanks in the position she will meet them
    on exam day.

    **The blanks are the point of this part.** A blank's wrong options must be grammatically
    fine and only contextually wrong: they fit the sentence, and they contradict something in
    the first email or clash with the reply's register. An obviously ungrammatical distractor
    turns the hardest question type in the section into a spot-the-typo exercise. This is
    harder to author than the correct answer and it should take the time it takes.

    Every comprehension question gets four options, an in-range key and an original
    explanation. Every blank gets its options, its key and an original explanation naming what
    in the first email or in the reply's tone rules the tempting alternative out — CELPIP-06
    requires per-item explanations and they roughly double the per-item authoring cost.

    Ids must be unique across the whole set, because question ids and blank ids share one
    answers map on the attempt. Prefix them by part so a collision is hard to write by accident.

    D-06 is absolute: every word original, written from the exam FORMAT only. Part names and
    item counts are the publicly documented structural facts this phase takes from third-party
    prep material; no sentence, stem, option or passage line may be carried across from any
    prep site or from study material the beta user owns. Open the file with the same
    originality header the shipped banks use.
  </action>
