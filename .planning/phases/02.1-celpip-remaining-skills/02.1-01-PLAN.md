<objective>
Prove the whole Phase 2.1 architecture on the thinnest possible path: **one** Speaking
prompt, recorded in the browser, played back, self-evaluated, and landed in Postgres
through the Phase 2 merge contract.

This is the tracer. It is deliberately the cheapest content in the phase (one ~60-word
scenario) and the most dangerous code in the phase (a CSP blocker that `next dev` cannot
surface, a browser API with no analog in this repo, and an extension to the merge algebra
that guards live production data). Everything after this plan is expansion outward from a
proven slice.

Purpose: retire the four architectural risks before a single word of Reading or Listening
content is authored — because content is the phase bottleneck and re-authoring it after an
architectural correction is the one cost the calendar cannot absorb.

Output: a working `/celpip/speaking/{promptId}` route, the `speakingAttempts` field
through all six lockstep sites of the persistence contract, and a reusable
attempt-merge helper the Listening and Reading plans will each add one line to.

**If execution stops right after this plan:** the learner can rehearse one CELPIP Speaking
Task 1 prompt end to end — timed prep, timed response, her own recording played back, and
a self-check — and it syncs to her account. It is reachable by direct URL only; plan 02
makes it discoverable from `/celpip`.
</objective>
