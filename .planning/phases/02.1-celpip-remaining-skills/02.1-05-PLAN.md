<objective>
Make Listening real: the hear-once, answer-after, explain-and-transcribe runner, wired to one
complete original part.

Purpose: this is the first plan in which the learner can practise the thing that cannot be
practised on paper — following speech she cannot re-read, under time pressure, with the
questions hidden until it is over. That is the whole reason D-04 and D-05 exist, and it is
the highest marginal-value content in the phase. The part chosen here is the news item: one
speaker, roughly 225 words, five questions — the cheapest complete part shape in the exam,
so the runner is proved end to end at the smallest possible authoring cost.

Output: a working `/celpip/listening/{setId}` route, `LISTENING_SETS` no longer empty, the
landing reporting real Listening coverage, and the content harness gaining the listening
invariants — including the per-turn word ceiling that keeps Chrome from silently cutting a
script in half.

**If execution stops right after this plan:** the learner has a complete, usable CELPIP
Listening drill in the real exam's shape, plus all eight Speaking task shapes. The landing
says truthfully that one of the six listening part shapes is available.
</objective>

  <action>
    Build `ListeningPlayer` as a client component with a phase machine one step longer than
    Writing's: brief, then playing, then answering, then results. The transition from playing
    to answering is driven by exactly one thing — the last utterance's `onend`, reported by
    the speech driver. Nothing else may reveal the questions (D-05).

    **Questions are revealed one at a time, in the order the audio covered them, and an
    answered question is not revisitable.** This is confirmed against the beta user's official
    format material and it is not a detail: being able to see all the questions at once, or to
    go back and change an earlier answer after a later one jogs your memory, makes the section
    easier than the exam and trains the wrong habit. Render one question, take the answer, then
    advance. Keep a visible position indicator so she knows how many remain. On submit, the
    results view shows all of them together with the key and the explanations — that is where
    review happens, not during the run.

    Before the brief phase arms anything, render `AudioCheck`. The timer does not start until
    the learner has confirmed she heard the warm-up line or has explicitly chosen to continue
    anyway. Reuse `Timer` unchanged — it is skill-agnostic, its countdown is anchored to a
    timestamp so it does not drift in a throttled background tab, it already handles
    `prefers-reduced-motion`, it turns vermilion under two minutes, and it offers pause in
    practice mode. Use its documented reset-on-changed-`totalSeconds` behaviour to re-arm
    between parts. Copy the sticky-header wrapper the Writing simulator puts around it. Put
    the two-way expiry exit on screen exactly as Writing does — submit as-is or continue
    untimed — and never discard her work on expiry.

    **Two things from the shipped `ClipPlayer` must not be copied.** It offers a "show
    transcript" button *before* answering, which violates D-04's hear-never-read; and it
    offers unlimited replay, which the real exam does not. Keep only the post-submit
    transcript reveal, rendered with speaker labels since the turns carry them.

    "Plays once" cannot actually be enforced — a page reload restarts everything, and
    pretending otherwise is worse than not trying. Enforce it inside the attempt lifecycle:
    the play control disables the moment the first utterance starts, and if a replay control
    is offered at all it increments a `replays` counter that is recorded on the attempt, so
    her own history stays honest with her. That is worth more than a lock she can defeat in
    one keystroke. If playback reports a failure, set `audioFailed` on the attempt and give
    her the transcript escape rather than a dead screen.

    Handle backgrounding: listen for `visibilitychange`, cancel on hide, and treat the attempt
    as needing an explicit restart rather than pretending playback continued — Safari can
    wedge its synthesizer when backgrounded mid-utterance until the page is reloaded.

    A note-taking area during playback: reuse the Writing textarea's class string and layout.
    Notes live in React state only and are shown in the results view within the session; they
    are not persisted, for the reason recorded in plan 04. Say so in one line of UI copy so
    she is not surprised. Practice mode pauses the timer but cannot pause the audio — state
    that in the UI too rather than letting it surprise her.

    Scoring is objective-key comparison client-side, the same one-line filter
    `ReadingRoom` already uses. Per D-02 no AI and no automated *judgement* is involved: an
    answer key is not scoring in the sense D-02 forbids. Show, per question, what she chose,
    what was correct, and the authored explanation. Reuse `ReadingRoom`'s option-button
    treatment verbatim — the same class strings, the same teal-for-correct and
    vermilion-for-wrong `color-mix` fills, the same disabled-after-submit rule.

    **Diverge from `ReadingRoom` on one point deliberately:** it disables submit until every
    question is answered. Do not. The house rule is never to block submission on a quality
    signal, and under exam conditions an unanswered question is a real outcome. Allow submit,
    score blanks as wrong.

    `finalizeAttempt` copies the Writing simulator's shape exactly: an early return on a
    `finalizedRef`, the transient state dropped, then the append through
    `addListeningAttempt`. Wire it to every exit path out of the results view — the retry
    button and the back link's onClick — and reset the ref on retry. This is Phase 1's
    hard-won lesson and it is named explicitly in the phase context.

    The route file mirrors the writing route exactly: a non-async `generateStaticParams` over
    `LISTENING_SETS`, an async page whose `params` is a Promise that is awaited, `notFound()`
    called rather than returned, no `"use client"` on the route, and the `JsonLd`
    LearningResource envelope with `teaches` set to CELPIP Listening.

    Design tokens only — no hard-coded hex, `--sky` as the CELPIP accent, teal for correct,
    vermilion for wrong, `font-display` headings, `rounded-[var(--radius)]` card panels with
    `shadow-[var(--shadow-soft)]`.

    Author the **news item** part: one speaker, roughly 225 words of original spoken-register
    prose, five questions testing factual recall — the item count and the part's roughly six-
    to seven-minute total (audio plus answering) are confirmed against the beta user's official

    segment, and questions each carrying a stem, four options, the correct index, and an
    explanation of why the right answer is right and — where it earns its length — why the
    most tempting wrong option is wrong. CELPIP-07 requires per-question explanations and
    they roughly double the per-item cost, so budget for them rather than discovering them.

    **The per-turn word ceiling is a correctness constraint, not a style note.** Chrome
    truncates a long utterance at roughly fifteen seconds with no error and sometimes no
    `onend` — which, under D-05, means the questions never appear. At the app's speaking rate
    of 0.95 against a nominal 150 words per minute, fifteen seconds is about 35 words. Author
    every turn at 25 words or fewer; the harness gates at 35. A single-speaker news item is
    still authored as a sequence of turns for this reason, all attributed to the same speaker
    name — the chunking is what protects playback, not the speaker count.

    Write plausible distractors. An obviously wrong option teaches nothing; the difficulty of
    this section is in options that are all defensible until you remember what was actually
    said.

    **D-06 is absolute.** Every word original, written from the exam FORMAT only — part
    names, item counts and lengths are publicly documented structural facts and are all this
    phase takes from third-party prep material. No sentence, question stem, option or script
    line may be carried across from any prep site or from any study material the beta user
    owns. Open the file with the same originality header the shipped banks use.

    Set `timeLimitMinutes` for the set from the part durations plus answering time, and
    comment that it is provisional while the set is incomplete and will be revised as parts
    are added.
