<objective>
Close the grammar half of CONT-01: all four scenario×grammar pairs get five questions written for
that scenario, and the derived coverage starts moving off zero.

Purpose: D-01 requires each of the fifty-two pairs to have its own exercise, written for that
scenario. Grammar is the cheapest skill in the phase — four pairs — and it is the one that satisfies
the ROADMAP's third success criterion for free: the existing quiz already reports its topic and
level to `recordAttempt`, which is exactly what populates weak topics. Today these four pairs render
nothing at all while a pill and the structured data promise them.

The route is the one RESEARCH and PATTERNS both recommend and the one that defuses the phase's
stated failure mode: author a scenario-keyed bank of the **existing** question shape and hand it to
the **existing** renderer. Inventing a new exercise shape per skill is where inconsistency across
fifty-two exercises comes from.

Output: `scenario-grammar.ts` with four pairs at D-04's ratified floor of five questions, the
grammar branch of the dispatch, and the skill pages and pills reporting written coverage rather
than declarations.

**What the learner gains if execution stops right after this plan:** four scenarios — small talk,
job interviews, professional emails and phrasal verbs — are practicable in grammar with questions
about that scenario, and the mistakes she makes there reach her weak spots. The other forty-eight
pairs still say plainly that they are not written yet.
</objective>
