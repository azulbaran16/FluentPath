<objective>
Close the writing half of CONT-01: all nine scenario×writing pairs get a prompt written for that
scenario — a task, a word range, a self-check list and an original model answer — rendered as a
single task rather than as a picker.

Purpose: these nine pairs render nothing at all today while a pill and the structured data promise
them. D-01 requires each to get its own exercise. The cheapest correct route, and the one that
defuses the phase's stated failure mode, is to author the **existing** prompt shape per scenario and
hand it to the **existing** desk, which already takes a plain array and knows nothing about
scenarios. The only component work is that the desk renders a prompt picker even for one prompt.

Output: `scenario-writing.ts` with nine prompts, the writing branch of the dispatch, and a desk that
drops its picker when there is nothing to pick.

**What the learner gains if execution stops right after this plan:** thirteen of the fifty-two pairs
are written — the four grammar pairs and all nine writing pairs — and every scenario that declares
writing can be practised with a task about that scenario instead of showing nothing. The reading and
speaking pairs still say they are not written yet.

No automated scoring is introduced. A writing prompt is self-evaluated against its checklist and its
model answer, exactly as the global writing room and the CELPIP writing section already are; real
correction waits for the tutor in Phase 5. Saying otherwise would be an overclaim.
</objective>
