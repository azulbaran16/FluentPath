<objective>
Open the speaking half of CONT-01 — the largest block in the phase at thirty of the fifty-two pairs
— with the rehearsal task shape and the first fourteen: all of Social & Everyday Life, all of Work &
Professional, and `academic/debate`.

Purpose: D-01 rejected shared routing precisely because of what happens to speaking today. Twenty-one
speaking pairs are handed a three-line generic set shared by every scenario in their world, so two
scenarios return the same exercise. CONT-02 has already given all thirty-five scenarios their own
phrases, which fixes the warm-up. What is still missing is the *exercise*: a concrete rehearsal task
that makes practising "complaining politely" practise complaining rather than pronunciation.

The task shape is deliberately small and uniform — a setup, three ordered moves, a success check —
because inconsistency across fifty-two exercises is this phase's named failure mode and speaking
carries the most of them.

It must work with no AI and no microphone. The tutor role-play is a clearly-labelled stub until an
API key is configured, and that is Phase 5's requirement, not this one; a speaking exercise that
depended on it would not be practicable today. The panel is a rehearsal brief the learner works
through aloud on her own, and the existing tutor step keeps its own place on the page.

Output: `scenario-speaking.ts`, the speaking task panel, the speaking branch of the dispatch, and
fourteen of the thirty speaking pairs written.

**What the learner gains if execution stops right after this plan:** every Social and Work scenario
can be rehearsed against a task written for it. The sixteen remaining speaking pairs still carry
their own phrases and vocabulary and say plainly that their rehearsal task is not written yet.

**This plan shares wave 8 with 03-08 and must not assert a global written total.** Both depend on
03-07 and neither depends on the other; their `files_modified` are disjoint, so they may merge in
either order. Fourteen speaking pairs land here and four reading pairs land in 03-08, which means
any "N of 52 written" assertion in either plan is really an assertion about which merged first.
Every gate here therefore counts **speaking** pending pairs, which is a statement about this plan's
own subject and is order-independent. For the record, the wave ends at 36 of 52 written whichever
way round it runs: eighteen from 03-07, fourteen from here, four from 03-08.
</objective>
