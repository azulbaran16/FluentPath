<objective>
Finish D-05 — every surface that counts or renders a due item now resolves it through the shared
resolver rather than the global grammar bank — and land the first thirteen scenarios of CONT-02:
all of Social & Everyday Life and all of Travel & Errands, six phrases and eight vocabulary cards
each.

Purpose: D-02 is binding. Phrases and vocabulary land across all thirty-five scenarios before the
fifty-two exercises, because those items feed the spaced-repetition queue and every scenario starts
contributing to the learning loop the day its items exist, even while its exercise is still missing.

plumbing between an authored item and the learner actually meeting it again.

Output: three review surfaces resolving across banks, a merge group proving the algebra holds for
scenario-shaped keys, and thirteen scenarios at D-04's ratified floors.

**What the learner gains if execution stops right after this plan:** everything she practises in the
Social and Travel worlds — thirteen of the thirty-five scenarios, including every travel scenario
she would use on a trip — has phrases and vocabulary written for it, and all of it now shows up in
her due count, her mistake notebook and her weak spots. The other twenty-two scenarios still say
plainly that their phrases are not written yet.
</objective>
