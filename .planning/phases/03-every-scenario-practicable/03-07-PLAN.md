<objective>
Open the reading half of CONT-01: make the single-passage reader reusable, give scenario
comprehension questions the explanation they have never had, and author the five A2 and B2 reading
pairs.

Purpose: nine pairs declare reading and render nothing at all today. D-01 requires each to get its
own passage. The existing reading room is a *browser* — a level filter and a list of texts — which
is the wrong shape for a learner who arrived at one scenario, and its single-passage reader is not
exported. That is the whole component gap. RESEARCH also names a real content gap in the existing
question shape: it has no explanation field at all, so a learner is told she was wrong and never
told why. Scenario questions must carry one, and it must be required rather than optional, because
the repo has already been bitten by an author forgetting an optional field.

Reading is the most expensive skill per pair in this phase — roughly 158 words each against 108 for
a writing prompt — which is why the nine are split across this plan and the next, with the four C1
passages kept together in plan 08.

A boundary worth stating rather than discovering: a scenario reading exercise scores itself and
explains its key, and does not write to the spaced-repetition queue. CONT-02's own wording is that
*phrases and vocabulary* feed the queue, and a comprehension question torn out of its passage cannot
be answered in a review card. This is a deliberate line, not an omission.

Output: an exported single-passage reader, a scenario reading bank with required explanations, and
five pairs written.

**What the learner gains if execution stops right after this plan:** eighteen of the fifty-two pairs
are written, and five scenarios — restaurants, the news, short stories, summarising and renting —
open straight into a text chosen for them with a key that explains itself.
</objective>
